Hindia

Indie Alternative Indonesian Pop

Hindia or Baskara Putra is an Indonesian indie artist known for creating meaningful work that resonates with everyday life. Blending indie pop and alternative styles with electronic elements, Hindia crafts melodies that are simple yet deeply emotional. His lyrics frequently explore themes such as life's anxieties, mental health, interpersonal relationships, and the search for self-identity. Through his music, Hindia transforms complex, hard-to-express feelings into a tapestry of words and melodies that touch the hearts of his listeners.

With a musical character that is honest, reflective, and relatable, Hindia has become an artist who resonates deeply with Generation Z. His songs represent the various struggles faced by young people—from social pressures and uncertainty about the future to the quest to find one's place in the world. Hindia's music is more than just entertainment; it serves as a space where Gen Z can feel understood, heard, and less alone in navigating life. Through his work, he encourages listeners to accept reality, make peace with themselves, and keep moving forward, even when the world feels overwhelming.
