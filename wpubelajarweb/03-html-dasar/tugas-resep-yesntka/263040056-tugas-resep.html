Recipe for Yeska's homemade chicken, egg, and rice🍗

Easy and Delicious homemade Chicken, Egg, and Rice dish that I made in my home when I'm still in High School at Jakarta. It uses less ingredients and simple to make even if you only can cook Indomie!!

What is a Chicken, Egg, and Rice dish?

Fried Chicken
The Fried Chicken is a chicken that was fried with hot oil and usually coated with flour
Eggs
The Egg is just a scrambled egg, you can use sunny side up style, but I recommended to make a scrambled egg
Rice
The Rice is just a normal asian rice, you can try to use a fried rice to make it much more tastier, but in this recipe, I only will do regular rice

Main Ingredients:

  • Chicken fillet [I recommended use thigh to make the chicken juicier]
  • Rice [regular rice and use any amount]
  • 2 Eggs

Small Ingredients:

  • Palm Oil/normal cooking oil
  • Chicken seasoning:
    • Bon Cabe 2-3 teaspoon [as much as you like]
    • Mushroom Stock [Kaldu Jamur bubuk] 1 teaspoon
    • Garlic Powder 1 teaspoon
  • Chicken batter:
    • Sprite/Carbonated Water (you can use normal water also) 1 small bottle/390ml
    • All Purpose Flour 300gr-400gr
    • Chicken seasoning 1 teaspoon [Optional]
  • Egg seasoning:
    • Salt 1 teaspoon
    • Black Pepper 1/2 teaspoon
    • Fish Sauce 1/2 teaspoon [Optional]

Steps:

    Fried Chicken
  1. Cut the chicken fillet into any shapes as you like or if you are lazy, no need to cut it [But I recommened to cut]
  2. Mix the Bon Cabe with Mushroom Stock and Garlic Powder
  3. Coat the chicken with the seasoning and let it rest on the fridge for atleast 15 minutes

    4. Now we gonna prepare the batter

  4. In a Bowl use 100gr-200gr of All Purpose Flour then mix it with 1 tablespoon of that chicken seasoning
  5. Pour Sprite little by little whille mix the flour batter also
  6. Make sure the batter is not too thick, if you want you can put more sprite in it

    7. Let's fry the chicken

  7. Prepare the oil in a pan or if you are Asian enough, use a WOK

    8. "fuiyoh nice"

    - Uncle Roger

  8. wait until the oil is hot, while waiting, coat the seasoned chicken with the flour batter that you have make
  9. Use the leftover dry flour as the final coating for the chicken

    10. 💡Pro Tips: To know when the oil is hot or not but you don't have a termometer, you can use a wooden spatula, if it's making a sizling noise, your oil is ready!

  10. Fry the chicken for 5-8 minutes with low-medium heat
  11. After that, let it cooled down for 5 minutes
  12. Then put it back with medium-high heat for 30 seconds or 1 minute
  13. After you done with the chicken, let it rest first
    Scrambled Egg
  1. Crack 2 eggs in a small bowl and put all the seasoning in the ingredient list
  2. Whisk the eggs until there are now leftover whites
  3. You can use the same pan as the chicken or use a small pan
  4. Use 1 teaspoon oil on the pan
  5. Turn the stove with low-medium heat

    6. If you want you eggs to be runny, quickly pour the eggs into the pan while it's still cold and whisk slowly

  6. Wait until the pan is quite hot then pour the eggs and whisk it
  7. whisk until the egg texture that you wants
  8. Set it aside
    Plating
  1. Took the rice from the rice cooker with the amount as you like
  2. Place the scramble eggs first on the rice
  3. Place the fried chicken on the top of the rice
  4. [Optional] Put some Bon Cabe on the egg or the rice

Congratulations! you've made a chicken with egg and rice!!
